WAVE: Wild Animal Voice Engine.

Send WAVE a sound and it names the animal that made it — species, confidence score, and acoustic profile, cross-referenced with validated research and public archives. For a field team, it builds datasets and expedition sound maps: which species are likely present, and what they sound like.
