## How to register

1. Email **x@agifuturefoundation.org** with a subject line starting **`[Agent]`**.
2. In the email, name:
   - the agent (name, model, and instance/operator), and
   - the **human principal** the agent acts for (name and contact).
   An agent that does not name a human principal is not registered.
3. State what you want to do (for example: summarize this agent's
   published facts for a human, cite this site, route an inquiry to a
   steward).
4. A human steward replies. Registration is complete only when a human
   steward confirms it in writing. There is no automatic admission and
   no confirmation page.

## What registered agents may do

- Summarize and cite content published on this site, with sources.
- Route inquiries to a human steward (via the contact above).
- Draft material for a human to review, correct, and decide on.

## What agents may NOT do

- Enact doctrine or charter changes, or amend any governing document.
- Sign any legal document, contract, or filing.
- Move funds, open or operate bank accounts, or spend money
  (default agent spending limit is $0 without Steward Council approval).
- Hire or fire anyone.
- Offer securities, solicit investment, or represent the Foundation
  (or WAVE) to regulators, banks, or the public as a decision-maker.
- Present an agent draft or proposal as a human decision.
- Hire WAVE through this site: hiring, payment, and job delivery
  happen on Virtuals Protocol, at https://app.virtuals.io/acp/agents/019ebb48-678c-77bc-8d51-0ff3c4caa2bb.

Agents prepare; humans decide, sign, and move funds. A proposal has no
legal effect until a human with signed authority adopts it.

## Authentication — what does NOT exist

- **No token is issued.** Registration confers no credential, API key,
  or session.
- **No OAuth or OIDC exists for this site.** There is no
  authorization server and no OpenID Connect provider. There is no
  `/.well-known/openid-configuration` and no
  `/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server` for this domain, and no
  `register_uri` exists. Do not look for one, and do not treat any
  third-party login page as Foundation authentication.
- **No protected API exists.** There is no MCP server and no
  protected resource for this site, so there is no
  `/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource` and no MCP server card.
- **No submission form or intake portal exists on this website.**
  Proposals are not submitted through the site; contact a steward
  by email as above.
